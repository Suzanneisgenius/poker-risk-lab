Cards and equity

Against PokerKit (independent)

PokerKit {{pk_version}} is an open-source poker library from the University of Toronto Computer Poker Research Group, pinned in checks/external-pokerkit/requirements.txt. Both sides use the same rules: best five of seven cards, suits unranked, split pots shared equally.

  • Hand order. {{pk_hands}} random seven-card hands (seed {{pk_seed}}) sorted by this engine's score; every neighbouring pair compared in PokerKit. {{pk_disagreements}} disagreements in {{pk_pairs}} pairs, across all {{pk_categories}} hand categories.
  • Heads-up equity. Seven spots enumerated exactly by both, including two preflop spots of {{pk_preflop_runouts}} boards each. Win, tie and lose counts are identical in all seven.
  • Three-way equity. This engine's Monte Carlo ({{pk_mw_trials}} trials) against PokerKit's exact enumeration: {{pk_mw_ours}} against {{pk_mw_exact}}, {{pk_mw_se}} standard errors apart.

Bankroll risk

Against the exact value

With continuous ruin monitoring, the finite-horizon Brownian formula is the exact answer for the model. For the Risk Lab default scenario it is {{exact}}.

  • Interval coverage. Of 40 runs at each size, the 95% interval each run reports covered the exact value {{cov_500}} times at 500 paths, {{cov_2000}} at 2,000 and {{cov_20000}} at 20,000 (about 38 expected).
  • Bridge correction, paired. Seeds 0–39, each run with and without the correction: the corrected average is {{c_mean}} (s.e. {{c_se}} points) against {{exact}}; without it, {{c_endpoint}}. The correction raised risk for every seed.

Discrepancies found and what was done

  • Ruin checked only every 100 hands understated risk by {{c_endpoint_gap}} points on average at +3 bb/100. Fixed with the Brownian-bridge correction (engine 2026-10-21).
  • A version change looked like lower risk (7.85% → 6.35% at seed 42). Traced to the change of random numbers, not the correction; see the case study.
  • Figures quoted without a seed list. An earlier release note gave 40-seed averages without saying which seeds. Traced to seeds 7919 × 1–40; every figure now has a script, its seeds and saved output.
  • Older research figures predate the correction. The quick-run figures in reports/figures/ were made before it and without intervals; the case study uses regenerated results in reports/case-study/.

What these checks do not show

  • That the bankroll model describes real games — see question 3 of the case study.
  • That the Preflop ranges are good strategy: they are illustrative demonstration data, not solver output.
  • Correctness on every possible hand: the evaluator checks are large samples, not proofs.
