    MethodRisk of ruin

    Methodology

    • Bankroll in big blinds, updated in steps of step hands.
    • Each step: increment ~ Normal(winrate·step/100, std·√(step/100)).
    • Ruin is absorbing: once bankroll ≤ threshold, the path is clipped there and cannot recover.
    • Risk of ruin = fraction of ruined paths; the confidence interval is the Wilson score interval.
    • Finite-horizon Brownian first-passage is the most comparable analytical benchmark; infinite-horizon gambler's ruin exp(-2μb/σ²) is an upper bound.

    Assumptions and limitations

    • i.i.d. results ignore session dependence, tilt, fatigue, and game selection.
    • Normal increments may understate heavy tails; win-rate estimates carry sampling uncertainty.
    • Deposits, withdrawals, and stake changes are excluded. Cash-game variance only — not tournament ICM.
    • Outputs are conditional model estimates, not guarantees.

    This standalone page uses its own seeded RNG, so results are reproducible here but will not byte-match the Python engine (same model, different random stream).
