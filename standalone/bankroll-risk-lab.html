// Research strands

STRAND 01 · NUMERICS

Barrier resolution & the Brownian-bridge correction

A discrete step lets a path cross the ruin boundary and recover between observations, biasing risk downward. At a 500-hand step the estimate understates ruin by 8.2%; a Brownian-bridge crossing correction cuts the discretization error to <1%.

STRAND 02 · MONTE CARLO ERROR

Convergence & interval coverage

Estimator error scales with the fitted log-log slope −0.55 ± 0.03, matching the O(N⁻¹ᐟ²) rate. Wilson intervals are checked for empirical coverage rather than assumed, with a reproducible sequential stopping rule.

STRAND 03 · UNCERTAINTY

Bayesian propagation of parameter uncertainty

The win rate is estimated, not known. Plugging in a point estimate reports 27.3% ruin; integrating over the parameter posterior raises it to 36.5% — a factor of ×1.34. Ignoring estimation error makes the model look far safer than it is.

STRAND 04 · ROBUSTNESS

Model-misspecification stress tests

The i.i.d.-Normal assumption is deliberately broken at a fixed edge and variance. Serial dependence AR(1) inflates ruin ×1.44 and regime-switching volatility ×1.20; variance-matched heavy tails and shocks are roughly neutral.
